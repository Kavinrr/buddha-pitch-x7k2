Logline

He must finally embrace the legacy he spent his life trying to escape.

When his deaf and mute daughter’s beloved dog is brutally poisoned by a local gang, a quiet North Chennai dubbing artist who has spent his life avoiding violence is pushed into a conflict that threatens his family. To protect the people he loves, Raja must finally embrace the boxing legacy he spent his life trying to escape.

Story

Two parts. One fall and one rise.

I

Part I

The story of worlds colliding

01

The opening

The film opens with Raja and Sindhu battered and bleeding, surrounded by men waiting for Karna, a feared gangster released on emergency bail to kill them. Sindhu lies beside Raja, fighting for her life. Raja begins to narrate how they ended up here.

02

Ten years earlier

Raja and Sindhu lie on the terrace, talking about life, their past and the future; Sindhu wants a daughter who could become a singer, or a son as calm and collected as Buddha.

03

A little brown-and-white puppy

Their daughter Maya is born deaf and mute. Intensely loved, she develops an extraordinary bond with animals. For her birthday, the family brings home a little brown-and-white puppy and names it Buddha.

04

Maya’s shadow

Buddha becomes Maya’s shadow, the family’s troublemaker, stealing food, following Raja’s Pulsar and quietly sitting beside Appa after dialysis.

05

The Rajamangalam gang

Their neighbourhood also belongs to the Rajamangalam gang, a Telugu-speaking outfit led by the feared Karna. During a hit, Karna’s right-hand man Stephen finds a Persian cat and gives her to his son-in-law Anbu.

06

The cat wanders in

The cat wanders into Raja’s home, where the family and Buddha bond with her, unaware of her origins.

07

Two days later

Buddha is dead. The neighbours tell the family what they believe caused it. With nothing to prove it and no wish to invite trouble, the family lets it go.

08

A strange little kingdom

Sindhu and Maya grow forlorn until Raja brings home two abandoned puppies, Ira and Mani. Their arrival restores the family’s joy. Ira finds a suitor in Bad Doggy; Mani gathers the neighbourhood dogs around him. But when this world crosses paths with Anbu, the fun turns dangerous, forcing Raja to confront who he really is.

II

Part II

The story of a legacy awakened

09

Tommy Gunner’s grandson

Raja was raised around boxing by his grandfather, Tommy Gunner Sundarraj, a feared boxer of 1960s Madras. After Tommy Gunner is murdered, Raja’s parents make him abandon the sport. He becomes a dubbing artist, building a quiet family life until it is threatened.

10

The fight comes home

Raja retaliates, injuring members of Karna’s gang and forcing the gangster to respond from prison. While Raja is away, the gang brings the fight to his home. The family fights back with unexpected allies, driving the attackers away at a devastating cost.

11

A quiet permission

Raja considers burying the fighter within him once more, even leaving the neighbourhood to protect what remains of his family. But at a profound moment, Sindhu remembers Appa’s words. To Raja, they become a message from the father who spent his life shielding Raja from violence — a quiet permission to fight.

12

The ride out

Raja and Sindhu ride out to confront Karna’s gang. The forgotten boxing world of Madras rises behind them for one final stand.

13

Karna is no mere myth

He proves more formidable than anyone imagined. With everything he loves at stake, Raja must embrace the legacy he spent his life trying to escape.

World

Villivakkam, North Chennai.

Buddha is set in the rapidly developing suburb of Villivakkam, North Chennai — a place where ponds, trees and animals give way to concrete and metro construction. Yet the old world continues to live alongside the new.

The street is an extension of the home. Children play outside, pets roam free, neighbours know each other’s lives, and street dogs, snakes, cows, buffaloes and cats are part of families and their stories.

World 1

The street

An extension of the home. Pets roam free, neighbours know each other’s lives.

World 2

Rajamangalam

Karna runs his Telugu-speaking gang from prison. Their faith in God runs deep, as does their loyalty to their own. They live by a code of pride, and faith.

World 3

Raja’s family

Deeply connected to each other and the world around them. A quiet, instinctive matriarchy runs through the family — and a hidden history: a lineage of fighters, buried violence and an inheritance Raja has spent years trying to leave behind.

One neighbourhood.
Many worlds.

And they never had to collide.

Until they do.

Sound

Gaana, Malayalam hip-hop, old blues, Telugu hits and unexpected international tracks sit alongside the sounds of the neighbourhood and the fight sequences.

  • Gaana
  • Malayalam hip-hop
  • Old blues
  • Telugu hits
  • International

Music drives the rhythm and personality of the action, making violence feel raw, edgy, chaotic, exhilarating or frightening depending on the moment.

The fight

Raja’s fighting style is entirely rooted in boxing. His footwork, movement, defence and attacks come from the sport.

The choreography can heighten this into something almost anime-like — rhythmic, inventive and visually striking while retaining the weight and vulnerability of a real body.
